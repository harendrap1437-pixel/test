          🇮🇳 Indian Speech Emotion Research (BERIS & Parler-TTS Feasibility)
        

        

          

            1. BERIS Indian Emotion Corpus: Covers 8 major Indic languages (Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Odia, Bengali, Punjabi, Marathi). Research indicates that Indian speech emotion features exhibit distinct F0 peak excursions in anger and surprise compared to western datasets (RAVDESS/IEMOCAP).
          

          

            2. emotion2vec+ Base Benchmark Findings: The 9.25 MB distilled ONNX model achieves an ultra-low ~8ms inference latency on CPU, enabling true realtime factor (<0.05). However, acoustic evaluation on Indian recordings reveals an acoustic bias towards higher formants as "happy" and flat contours as "neutral", underscoring the need for BERIS fine-tuning.
          

          

            3. Expressive Indic TTS (Parler-TTS Hinglish Feasibility): En1gma02/Parler-TTS-Hinglish-Accent-Emotions provides text-conditioned emotion prompts. While high in fidelity, the 600M+ architecture requires ~2.4GB VRAM/RAM, making distilled VITS/Piper prosody modulation the recommended lightweight baseline for our 8GB RAM Android target.
